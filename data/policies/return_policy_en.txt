Return Policy

We want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. If something isn't right, this page explains how our return process works, what condition items need to be in, and which categories have special rules.

Standard Return Window
Most items can be returned within 14 days of the delivery date, for a full refund or exchange. The return window starts from the date the courier marks the order as delivered, not the order date. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition you received them, with all tags and accessories included.

How to Start a Return
To start a return, contact our support team with your order number and the reason for the return. We will provide a return authorization and instructions for sending the item back, including whether a courier will collect it from you or whether you need to drop it off at a designated location. Returns sent without a return authorization may experience processing delays.

Refund Process
Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider. Shipping fees from the original order are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defect or an error on our part.

Category-Specific Return Rules
Electronics can be returned within 14 days but must be in like-new condition with all original packaging, cables, and manuals; opened software or activated devices with usage history may only be eligible for a partial refund or store credit. Clothing items must be unworn and unwashed, with original tags attached; swimwear and undergarments are not eligible for return for hygiene reasons. Beauty products can only be returned if unopened and unused, due to health and safety regulations. Grocery items are not eligible for return once delivered, except in cases of spoilage or incorrect items received. Books are returnable within 14 days as long as they are undamaged, without writing or highlighting. Toys must be returned in original, unopened packaging. Automotive parts must be uninstalled and in resellable condition, with proof that they were not fitted to a vehicle. Sports equipment that has been used cannot be returned unless defective.

Damaged or Incorrect Items
If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. In these cases, we cover all return shipping costs and will send a replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.

Exchanges
If you would like a different size, color, or model, you can request an exchange instead of a refund when starting your return. Exchanges are subject to the same condition requirements as refunds and depend on the availability of the replacement item.

Non-Returnable Items
Certain items cannot be returned under any circumstances, including opened Beauty products, Grocery items (except in cases of quality issues), and any item marked as "Final Sale" at the time of purchase. These exceptions are always clearly noted on the product page before you complete your purchase.
