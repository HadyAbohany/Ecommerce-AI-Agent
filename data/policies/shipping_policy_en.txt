Shipping Policy

We aim to get every order to you as quickly and reliably as possible. This page explains our shipping timelines, costs, coverage areas, and how to track your order once it has been placed.

Processing Time
Every order is processed within 1 to 2 business days after payment is confirmed. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day. During high-demand periods such as seasonal sales, processing may take up to 3 business days. You will receive an email confirmation as soon as your order has been processed and handed over to the courier.

Delivery Times by Region
Delivery times vary depending on your location and the courier service assigned to your area. Standard delivery within major cities typically takes 2 to 4 business days from the day the order is processed. Deliveries to smaller towns and more remote areas may take 4 to 7 business days. For international orders, delivery generally takes between 7 and 14 business days, depending on customs processing in the destination country. Please note that customs delays are outside of our control and may extend these estimates.

Shipping Costs
Shipping costs depend on the weight and dimensions of your order, as well as your delivery location. A flat shipping fee is calculated automatically at checkout before you confirm your order, so you will always know the exact cost before payment. Orders above a certain value may qualify for free standard shipping; this threshold is displayed at checkout and may change during promotional periods.

Category-Specific Shipping Notes
Some product categories have special shipping considerations. Large Home items such as furniture may require scheduled delivery and could take longer than the standard estimate due to their size. Automotive parts and accessories are shipped with additional packaging to prevent damage in transit, which may add one extra business day to processing. Grocery items are shipped separately from other categories to maintain freshness and are only available for delivery within supported regions. Electronics and Beauty products are shipped in tamper-evident packaging for your protection.

Order Tracking
Once your order has shipped, you will receive a tracking number by email and SMS. You can use this number to follow your package's journey in real time through the courier's tracking page. If you do not receive a tracking number within 3 business days of placing your order, please contact our support team.

Failed or Delayed Deliveries
If a delivery attempt fails because no one was available to receive the package, the courier will typically attempt delivery again the next business day, and may leave a notice with further instructions. If your order is significantly delayed beyond the estimated delivery window, please reach out to our support team with your order number so we can investigate with the courier on your behalf.

International Shipping
We currently ship to a limited number of countries outside our home market. Availability is shown at checkout based on your delivery address. International orders may be subject to customs duties, taxes, or import fees that are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the checkout price. Please check your local regulations before placing an international order, especially for restricted categories such as certain Electronics or Automotive parts.
