Frequently Asked Questions

This page answers the questions we receive most often about payments, orders, and shopping with us. If you can't find what you're looking for here, our support team is happy to help.

Payment Methods
We accept major credit and debit cards, as well as popular local payment methods available in your region at checkout. Cash on delivery is available in select regions for orders below a certain value; this option will appear at checkout if you are eligible. All online payments are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways, and we never store your full card details on our servers.

Order Confirmation and Changes
Once you place an order, you will receive a confirmation email with your order number and a summary of your purchase. If you need to change your shipping address, add or remove items, or cancel your order, please contact support as soon as possible. We can usually accommodate changes if the order has not yet been processed for shipping; once an order has shipped, changes are no longer possible and you would need to use our Return Policy instead.

Order Cancellations
You can cancel an order free of charge as long as it has not yet been marked as shipped. To cancel, contact our support team with your order number. If the order has already shipped, you can still refuse delivery or initiate a return once it arrives, following our standard Return Policy.

Tracking Your Order
Every shipped order includes a tracking number sent to you by email and SMS, which you can use to follow its progress. If your order status hasn't updated in several days or the tracking information looks incorrect, please reach out to support with your order number.

Multiple Items in One Order
If your order includes multiple items, they may occasionally ship separately, especially if items come from different categories, such as combining a Grocery item with an Electronics item, or if one item is temporarily on backorder. You will be notified if your order is split into multiple shipments, and you will not be charged extra shipping for this.

Out-of-Stock Items
If an item you want is out of stock, you can often sign up for a restock notification on the product page. Occasionally, an item may become unavailable after you've placed an order; in this case we will contact you immediately to offer a substitute, a refund for that item, or a delay until restocked, according to your preference.

Contacting Support
Our support team can be reached through the contact form on our website, by email, or through live chat during business hours. Please include your order number in any support request so we can assist you more quickly. For warranty or return-related questions, see our Warranty Policy and Return Policy pages for full details before contacting us, as many common questions are already answered there.

Account and Order History
Creating an account lets you view your full order history, track current orders, and save your shipping details for faster checkout in the future. You can also check out as a guest without creating an account, though you will need your order number and email to track that order or request support afterward.

International Customers
We currently support shipping to a limited set of countries, shown at checkout based on your address. Prices are displayed in the currency relevant to your region where supported. Import duties, taxes, and customs fees for international orders are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the product price or shipping cost.
