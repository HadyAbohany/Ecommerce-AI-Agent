Warranty Policy

Many of our products come with a manufacturer or store warranty that protects you against defects in materials or workmanship. This page explains warranty lengths by category, what is covered, and how to make a warranty claim.

General Warranty Terms
A warranty covers defects that occur under normal use during the warranty period. It does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, unauthorized repairs, normal wear and tear, or exposure to conditions outside the product's intended use (such as water damage to a non-waterproof electronic device). Proof of purchase, such as your order confirmation or invoice, is required for any warranty claim.

Warranty Length by Category
Electronics generally carry a 12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects in components such as batteries, screens, and internal hardware. Home appliances and larger Home category items typically carry a 24-month warranty, reflecting their expected longer lifespan. Automotive parts and accessories carry a 6-month warranty, since they are subject to significant wear from regular vehicle use. Sports equipment carries a 6-month warranty against structural defects, though normal wear from use is not covered. Toys carry a 3-month warranty against manufacturing defects; damage from normal play is not covered. Office Products such as printers and electronics-adjacent office equipment carry a 12-month warranty similar to general Electronics. Clothing, Beauty products, Grocery items, and Books are not covered by a warranty, as they are consumable or single-use in nature; any issues with these categories are instead handled through our standard Return Policy.

Making a Warranty Claim
To make a warranty claim, contact our support team with your order number, a description of the issue, and photos or a short video showing the defect if applicable. Our team will review the claim and may ask you to send the item in for inspection. If the claim is approved, we will repair the item, replace it with the same or an equivalent model, or issue a refund, depending on the situation and the availability of parts or replacement stock.

What Voids a Warranty
A warranty becomes void if the product has been opened or repaired by someone other than an authorized service provider, if the original proof of purchase cannot be provided, if the defect resulted from misuse or an accident, or if the product was used for purposes other than its intended use. Removing or damaging any warranty seal or serial number sticker also voids coverage.

Extended Warranty Options
For select Electronics and Home appliance categories, an extended warranty may be purchased at checkout to extend coverage beyond the standard warranty period. Details and pricing for extended warranty options, when available, are shown on the individual product page.
